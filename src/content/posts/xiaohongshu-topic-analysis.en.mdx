> Most people start a new Xiaohongshu (RedNote) account by posting every day. But before you publish your first note, you should do a niche analysis and understand the competitive landscape.

There's a popular video going around about how new accounts should forget about recommendation-feed traffic and focus on search traffic instead. The core argument is sound — search traffic converts 3–5x better than feed traffic, lasts 6+ months, and isn't affected by throttling. The video covers how to find long-tail keywords and embed them in your content. But it skips one critical question: before you start digging for keywords, how do you know whether a keyword is even worth pursuing?

Here's how most new creators approach Xiaohongshu: pick a niche → browse what competitors are posting → imitate their content → pivot when results are bad. The fundamental problem with this workflow is that every decision is based on gut feeling.

The competitor posts you happen to see are just the tip of the iceberg. You don't know what the overall competitive landscape looks like, how wide the gap is between top-performing and average content, or whether there's actually room for a new account to break through.

That's exactly what niche analysis solves: before you write a single note, use data to map out the playing field.

## What a Xiaohongshu Niche Analysis Reveals

Let's use a concrete example. Say you're planning to create content around incense sticks — maybe you run a small shop, maybe you're managing content for a client, or maybe it's just a personal interest you want to explore.

You type "incense sticks" into the Xiaohongshu search bar and scroll through a few pages of results. The content is all over the place: atmospheric videos of Tibetan incense ceremonies, product reviews, meditation and sleep-aid content, business opportunity breakdowns.

But scrolling through a few pages only gives you a vague impression. The insights that actually matter require a more systematic analysis.

Questions like these are nearly impossible to answer by intuition alone: What kind of engagement can top-performing content in this niche achieve? What does average content look like? Which content angles dominate? Which direction gives a new account the best shot at gaining traction?

This angle taps into the mental burnout pain point, positioning incense as a tool for stress relief or meditation. **Honest Reviews & Buyer's Guides** makes up 13% — 8 posts averaging 450 likes. People searching for this content have clear purchase intent — they want to find good value products and avoid low-quality ones. Keywords like "avoid these mistakes," "beginner's guide," and "honest review" naturally attract high-intent traffic, and the creative bar is low — just be genuine and objective.

**Business Analysis & Side Hustle** sits at 8% — 5 posts averaging 300 likes, in the low tier. The audience is narrow, mostly people looking for side income opportunities. You need real industry depth to make this content worthwhile; otherwise it comes across as empty.

## The Decision Logic Behind the Data

Lay out all four directions side by side and the decision becomes clear.

If you're a brand-new account, the Eastern Aesthetics direction looks great on paper but has a terrible effort-to-result ratio. The Business Analysis direction has too narrow an audience — unless you have insider industry knowledge, it's hard to produce genuinely valuable content.

The remaining two directions are both viable entry points for new accounts. The Emotional Wellness angle has the advantage of higher average engagement and mature content templates — ideal for creators aiming for organic traffic. The Reviews & Guides angle has lower average likes but much stronger search intent — a better fit for accounts focused on product sales or building a highly targeted following.

The trending sub-keywords for each direction differ too. The wellness angle revolves around sleep aid, stress relief, meditation, and home comfort. The reviews angle revolves around authenticity verification, common pitfalls, and value-for-money. Which path to take depends on your positioning, your product, and how you plan to monetize.

## FAQ

### How do I choose the right content direction for a new Xiaohongshu (RedNote) account?

Search the main keyword for the niche you're planning to enter and look at the content distribution and organic reach potential in the niche report. Prioritize mid-tier directions with high organic reach potential, and avoid top-tier directions where the barrier to entry is extremely high.

### As a content agency, how do I quickly assess the competitive landscape of an unfamiliar industry on Xiaohongshu?

Search the industry's main keyword and look at three metrics: upper-quartile average likes (the ceiling for top content), median likes (the baseline for average content), and the drop-off percentage between them. The larger the drop-off, the bigger the gap between top and average content.
