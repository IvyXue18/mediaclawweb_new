MediaClaw starts at the browser, but the important work happens after capture.
Posts, comments, media, source context, and review notes need to stay attached
to an account, an activation code, and a credit ledger that operations can
inspect later.

## The capture path

The extension handles in-browser collection. The website owns account identity,
activation-code status, billing, credit history, and operational repair. Keeping
those surfaces connected avoids hidden plugin-local state.

## The operating path

Operators can review users, credits, payments, activation codes, referral
records, and partner channels from the admin backend. That makes failed payment
fulfillment, credit disputes, and channel handoffs visible enough to repair.

## Why it matters

Capture tools are only useful when the downstream workflow is accountable.
MediaClaw treats captured evidence, paid entitlements, and partner attribution
as one chain instead of separate screenshots, spreadsheets, and manual notes.
