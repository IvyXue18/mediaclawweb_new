# MediaClaw sign-in visual system

Use the official sign-in surface as the default family, not as a pixel-for-pixel
template.

- Canvas: warm near-white `#fbfafc`
- Ink: slate `#0f172a`
- Muted copy: `#64748b`
- Card: white with a subtle cool-gray border
- Accent: cyan-to-violet-to-pink, used sparingly
- Geometry: large rounded panels, pill labels, compact browser chrome
- Depth: soft slate shadow, thin translucent borders, layered product captures
- Typography: bold compact sans for the promise; italic serif only for the
  decisive phrase
- Composition: left copy takes roughly 43%; right product story takes 57%
- Product story: two or three real screenshots, offset and overlapped, with one
  small result badge or connector

Avoid neon sci-fi backgrounds, generic 3D blobs, fake dashboards, dense charts,
and full-canvas gradients. The result should feel like MediaClaw's sign-in page:
calm editorial copy on the left, credible product evidence on the right.
