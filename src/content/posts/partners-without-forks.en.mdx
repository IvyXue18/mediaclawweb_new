Partner channels often become expensive when each channel asks for a separate
package, brand, or handoff process. MediaClaw keeps the product on one backend
and moves attribution into data instead.

## One backend, many channels

Partner records carry partner codes, owner accounts, contract status, variant
IDs, seat limits, and operational notes. Orders and activation codes can then
carry the same attribution without a separate deployment.

## Export for handoff

When a supplier needs a batch of activation codes, operations can export the
partner-linked credentials. The export is useful for reconciliation, while the
website keeps the source of truth.

## Keep the boundary clear

White-label work should not become a fork unless the business truly needs a
separate product. For most channels, data-level attribution is easier to audit,
repair, and keep compatible with future backend changes.
